Test hooks load only in the `VITE_E2E_HOOKS` build. `--gallery-tile-size` and the Toast `action` option are registered in `docs/design-system.md`.
- **Other features' e2e specs:** the edits to adjust, crop-rotate, draw, export, open-and-view and undo-redo and to `e2e/harness.ts` keep every original assertion.
